1. Who we are

Snowplow Hero is published by Korsika Studio, a sole proprietorship registered at 47 rue Vivienne, 75002 Paris, France. For any question about your data: bertonba62@gmail.com.

2. Data handled by the game

Snowplow Hero does not ask for an account, a name or an email address, and has no server. Your progress (money, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and funded by ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its ad partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location;
  • technical information about the device (model, operating system, language);
  • your interactions with ads (views, clicks).

Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch them. To learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent window appears on first launch. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes and partners in detail. If you refuse, non-personalized ads may still be shown.

You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings, then "Privacy and ads".

Legal bases: your consent for personalized advertising and for access to information on your device; legitimate interest for some purposes (measurement, security, fraud prevention), which you can object to from the same window.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Google and some of its partners may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses, EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

6. Retention

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Advertising data is kept by Google and its partners according to their own policies.

7. Children

Snowplow Hero is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about children under 13.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw your consent. Since the game keeps no data on a server, most of these rights can be exercised directly in the game (Settings) or with Google. For any request: bertonba62@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL): www.cnil.fr.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated, for example if new services are added to the game. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
